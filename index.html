About me

Freelancer. Builder by habit.

See my work

I'm Krish Gupta, a freelancer and AI developer with a B.Sc. Data Science background, passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science and Full-Stack Development. With 2 years of experience and 110+ projects, I love building innovative solutions that solve real-world problems. My goal is to turn ideas into impactful technology.

Most of my time goes into LLMs, NLP, generative AI, analytics and workflow automation. Right now I'm a Data Science Intern at Unified Mentor, and I'm building two things of my own: Justice for All, an AI legal-help platform for people who can't easily reach a lawyer, and YourNeed AI, which helps students and small businesses get AI-ready.

Outside the code I've coordinated a whole region of IIT Madras BS students, run an event at Paradox'25, and won a quiz at Paradox'24. I speak Hindi, Marathi and English, and I'm happiest when a messy problem finally becomes a clean pipeline.
